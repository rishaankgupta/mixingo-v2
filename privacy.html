PRIVACY

Private beta means simple data rules.

This is a pre-launch privacy summary for waitlist collection. It should be reviewed by counsel before public launch or production API processing.

What we collect

Name, email, selected use case, and any message you send us. The waitlist also records submission time, IP-based country, region and city, ISP, browser, operating system, device type, browser language, timezone, screen size, referrer, and landing page.

Why we collect it

To manage beta access, understand where and how the waitlist is used, prioritize language coverage, invite developer testers, and send useful product updates.

What we do not sell

We do not sell waitlist data. We do not use submitted waitlist details for third-party advertising.

Waitlist services

The beta page can contact ipapi.co for IP-based location metadata, then submit the form and metadata through Google Apps Script to a Google Sheet controlled by Mixingo.

Translation data & files

Production retention rules are not final. Before general availability, they will explicitly cover uploaded originals, extracted text, generated translated files, and deletion timing.

Contact

Email hello@mixingo.dev for access, deletion, or privacy questions.

WHY MIXINGO

Nobody taught you to mix languages.
Every translator since has punished you for it.

Multilingual people switch languages because it's faster, warmer, and more precise. Mixingo exists for that reality.

OUR THESIS

Narrow on purpose

We are not building every camera, speech, and enterprise localization workflow on day one. We focus on one hard job: translating code-mixed language naturally, whether it arrives as pasted text, a PDF, DOCX, TXT, or ebook file, or a text or file request through the API.

Preserve the mix

When someone says "yaar chill kar" they're not saying "friend, please relax." They're saying it with warmth and familiarity. Generic tools strip all of that out. We don't.

Web + API

The private-beta web translator handles pasted text and complete files. The API provides the same full product access — text plus PDF, TXT, DOCX, and ebook translation — for developers building it directly into their workflows.

Honest about launch stage

Mixingo is pre-launch. The promise must be earned through benchmarks, public examples, disclosed misses, and feedback from multilingual users who know when a line sounds wrong.

Global by design

Code-mixing is not only an India problem. The pattern appears anywhere multilingual people live online: English with Hindi, Spanish, Filipino, Arabic, Malay, Tamil, French, German, and more.

Human over literal

A translation can be technically correct and still useless. The product standard is simple: would a real person understand the meaning without cringing?

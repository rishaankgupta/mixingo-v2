AN HONEST COMPARISON

Google does a thousand things.
We do one thing they don't.

Google Translate, DeepL and Azure Translator are mature, powerful products. Mixingo is not claiming to replace them. We're building for the messy multilingual words they weren't designed around — in sentences, APIs, and complete documents.

Important: “not an explicitly documented product focus” does not mean a competitor will always fail on every mixed sentence. Output varies by language pair, wording, model and product version. Document-layout fidelity also varies with source-file complexity. Mixingo’s differentiation is its stated design target, which must still be proven through transparent testing.

THE BRUTALLY HONEST VERSION

Use Google when…

You need massive language reach, camera input, speech, offline use, or a mature cloud ecosystem.

Use DeepL when…

You need polished, conventional translation with style controls across supported European and Asian languages.

Use Azure when…

You need enterprise-scale translation workflows, custom models, or deep Microsoft cloud integration.

Try Mixingo when…

Your words mix languages — in one sentence or a complete document — and stiff output is not good enough. Both the private-beta web translator and API support PDF, DOCX, TXT, and ebook files.

Our document limitation

Straightforward files should stay near-original. Dense columns, overlapping layers, scanned pages, unusual fonts, and heavily designed PDFs can shift; text baked into images may remain unchanged. Complex layouts are preserved on a best-effort basis.

How we earn trust

Mixingo is pre-launch. We earn trust through reproducible examples, disclosed misses, no absolute claims, and letting multilingual users judge whether the output sounds human.
